I am a Research Scientist at Google Research, working on Gemini post-training. Before that I was a visiting postdoctoral researcher at Google (2024–2025). I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Washington in 2024, where I was advised by Prof. [Yin Tat Lee](https://yintat.com/).
Prior to that, I earned a B.S. in Mathematics and Physics from Tsinghua University in 2020.

My current work centers on post-training for large language models — data curation and synthesis for RL and SFT, evaluation methodology for reasoning and agentic capabilities, and multi-agent systems. I also continue to work on differential privacy, including [Urania](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04681), a differentially private framework for insights into chatbot use, and [VaultGemma](https://research.google/blog/vaultgemma-the-worlds-most-capable-differentially-private-llm/), an open 1B-parameter LLM pretrained with formal privacy guarantees.

My earlier research is in theoretical computer science and machine learning, with an emphasis on differential privacy, stochastic optimization, and algorithm design.
